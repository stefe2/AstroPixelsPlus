# Music choreographies (`:MU`)

The Kyber plays a song and sends `:MUnn` to AstroPixels at the same time. AstroPixels then runs a
choreography generated from that exact MP3: panels, logics, PSI and holos follow the music.

- The song title scrolls in white on the rear logic, then the rear logic joins the choreography.
- Random holo moves (`*HA`, `*HV`) pause during the song and resume after it.
- Any other sequence command stops the song; `:MU00` stops it too. The droid is then reset:
  panels closed, logics and PSI back to normal, holos centered.
- `:MU99` is a 20 s test choreography, without music.

## How a choreography is built

`tools/music/build.py` analyses each MP3 (Python, `librosa`), then applies fixed rules. Nothing is
written by hand, and every song gets the same treatment.

### Analysis

| Measure | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Tempo, beats, bars (groups of 4 beats) | timing of every move |
| Bass hits (kick) and treble hits (snare, cymbals) | panel pops |
| Bass, mid and treble energy, overall loudness | intensity of each moment |
| Dominant note of each bar, placed on the circle of fifths | colors: related chords give related colors |
| Left/right balance (stereo files) | where the holos look |
| Sections (verse, chorus, break…) | changes of style |

Every section gets an intensity from 0 to 3, relative to the rest of the song. The peak level (3) is
kept for at most about a third of the song, so it stays special.

### Rules by intensity

| Intensity | Logics and PSI | Holos | Panels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — calm | color of the harmony, changing with the chords | one holo at a time turns slowly toward the side the sound comes from (stereo), else a slow scan; soft color pulse | closed |
| 1 — groove | halves flip-flop at the tempo; PSI flash at the tempo | one holo at a time looks left or right on each bar; solid color of the bar | the dome "breathes": one panel type half-opens on strong bars, in turn |
| 2 — energy | front and rear swap two colors on every beat | all holos nod every other beat; color swaps on the beat | pie panels pop on bass hits, small and medium panels on snare backbeats, top panel every other bar |
| 3 — peak | fire or rainbow (alternating from one peak to the next); PSI rainbow | all holos nod on every beat; rainbow | **equalizer**: the number of panel types open follows the bass, from the mini panels up to the top panel |

### Transitions

- **Build-up**: during the 4 bars before a big jump in intensity, all panels creep open (10 %, 20 %,
  30 %, 40 %), the logics flash faster and faster, and the holos rise. On the last beat everything goes
  dark, then the peak starts with all panels opening at once. The rules know the whole song in
  advance, so the droid anticipates the drop instead of reacting to it.
- **Break** (quiet part right after a loud one): lights out, panels closed, holos centered.
- **End**: panels close, holos center, then the top panel tips like a hat on the last notes.

### Mechanical limits enforced

- Panel moves last at least 150 ms, and the same panel type is not commanded twice within 250 ms.
- A holo does not move twice within 300 ms.
- A logic display is not changed twice within 110 ms.
- The rear logic is left alone while the title scrolls.

`tools/music/validate.py` checks every generated choreography against these limits and the command
formats. The build stops if one fails.

## Adding or changing a song

1. Copy the MP3 the Kyber plays into `mp3/` (never committed: see `.gitignore`).
2. Add an entry in `tools/music/songs.json`: `mu` (the `:MUnn` number, 01–98), `file`, `title`
   (shown on the rear logic: capitals, digits, space, `!`, `-`, `.`), `offset_ms`, `enabled`.
3. Run `python tools/music/build.py` from the repository root (first time: `pip install librosa`).
   Analyses are cached in `mp3/_cache/`.
4. Check the result in `mp3/preview.html` (see below), then build and flash the firmware.
5. On the Kyber, make the song button send `:MUnn` together with the sound.

### Timing offset

If the droid is early or late compared with the music played by the Kyber, set `offset_ms` for that
song: a positive value delays the choreography, a negative value advances it. Then regenerate and flash.

### Preview

`mp3/preview.html` plays each MP3 with a top view of the dome: open panels, logic and PSI colors,
holo direction and color, and a timeline of events (click it to seek). Open it in a browser from the
`mp3/` folder. It is generated with the choreographies and stays local, like the MP3 files.
